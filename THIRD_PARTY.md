the unmodified official2.0.30 archive and verifies SHA256; its license and
documentation remain in that archive/installation. No AutoHotkey executable
is committed or embedded into this repository's release.

- [AutoHotkey source and license](https://github.com/AutoHotkey/AutoHotkey)

  NVIDIA driver's library; no NVIDIA binaries distributed.
- Windows shell, Win32 and PowerShell are provided by Windows.
- llama.cpp and Ollama are independently installed software; no runtime or
